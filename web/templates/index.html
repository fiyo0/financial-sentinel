Financial Sentinel • System Key & Intelligence Guide Reference Manual

Comprehensive guide to color coding, directional indicators, alert priority tiers, catalyst sources, feedback mechanics, and quantitative risk metrics.

🎨

Ticker Impact Signals & Color Coding

Every detected market event is analyzed by our multi-agent pipeline and assigned a directional impact score and confidence-weighted percentage move. The card border, badge color, and mathematical sign communicate the direction:

🟢 BULLISH (+X.X%) BULLISH (+3.5%)

Positive Upside Catalyst: Indicates news, earnings beats, contract awards, margin expansions, or regulatory clearances expected to exert upward pricing momentum on the holding. Prefixed with a + sign in emerald green.

🔴 BEARISH (-X.X%) BEARISH (-2.8%)

Downside Risk Threat: Indicates guidance cuts, margin compression, customer churn, supply shortages, litigation, or macroeconomic headwinds exerting downward pressure. Prefixed with a - sign in rose red.

🟡 HIGH VOLATILITY (±X.X%) HIGH_VOLATILITY (±4.0%)

Two-Sided / Binary Outcome: Indicates high-dispersion events such as FDA drug approval verdicts, major patent trial rulings, or impending FOMC rate decisions where significant movement is expected in either direction. Prefixed with ± in amber.

🔵 NEUTRAL (~X.X%) NEUTRAL (~0.5%)

Negligible / Balanced Impact: Background noise or routine disclosures that produce balanced or immaterial price movement (<1.0%). Prefixed with ~ in cyan/slate.

⚡

Alert Urgency Tiers & The "P0 Critical" Badge

Important: Urgency is independent of Direction! A stock can have an urgent bullish breakout catalyst or an urgent bearish crash risk. Alert priority reflects the magnitude and required attention level:

⚡ P0 CRITICAL Immediate Action Required
Impact Magnitude > 3.0%

High-magnitude catalyst requiring immediate portfolio evaluation. Triggered when expected price movement exceeds 3.0% or directly impacts a concentrated holding (>15% portfolio weight).

Examples of How It Appears:
• Bullish P0: ⚡ P0 CRITICAL + BULLISH (+4.8%) → Urgent positive earnings surge.
• Bearish P0: ⚡ P0 CRITICAL + BEARISH (-5.2%) → Urgent downside collapse risk.
📌 P1 NOTABLE Impact Magnitude: 1.5% – 3.0%

Substantial development affecting a holding, sector competitor, or supply chain partner. Worth reviewing during standard market hours.

📋 P2 WATCHLIST Impact Magnitude: < 1.5%

Background trend, peripheral commentary, or long-dated macro theme monitored by the discovery radar.

"Info Source" Catalyst Vectors

The ℹ️ Info Source badge displayed on each ticker card explains the transmission vector through which an economic shock or news catalyst influences your holding:

📰 Direct Market News

Primary source filings directly from the company: SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K), official press releases, earnings conference call transcripts, or major financial wire dispatches.

🔗 Supply Chain Ripple

Indirect upstream supplier or downstream customer catalysts. For example, wafer pricing changes at TSMC impacting Nvidia/Apple margins, or ASML export curbs impacting semiconductor equipment.

📈 Macro Multiple / Rates

Broader economic variables: Federal Reserve interest rate expectations, 10-year Treasury yield moves, CPI/PPI inflation surprises, oil prices, or currency fluctuations that reset market valuation multiples.

🤖 Quant Engine / Factor Alert

Algorithmic triggers generated by quantitative risk models: statistical correlation breaks, beta expansion, volatility regime switches, or sudden volume and options open-interest anomalies.

Quantitative Risk Metrics Guide

Real-time mathematical risk metrics computed across your holdings and cash balances using historical covariance matrices:

📉 Value at Risk (VaR 95%, 1-Day) Parametric Covariance

The maximum dollar and percentage loss expected over a single trading day at a 95% statistical confidence interval under normal market conditions. (e.g., a $1,500 VaR means there is only a 5% chance the 1-day drop exceeds $1,500).

⚖️ Portfolio Beta Benchmark: S&P 500 (1.0)

Measures systematic sensitivity relative to the broader market. A beta of 1.25 indicates the portfolio is expected to move 25% more aggressively than the S&P 500; a beta of 0.8 indicates defensive, lower-volatility behavior.

📊 Sharpe Ratio Risk-Adjusted Return

Evaluates excess portfolio return generated per unit of total risk (standard deviation) above the risk-free rate. >1.0 is considered solid; >2.0 indicates exceptional risk-adjusted performance.

🎯 Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) Concentration Metric

Measures asset concentration by summing the squared percentage weights of all holdings. <1,500 reflects healthy diversification; >2,500 flags heavy single-stock exposure risk.

🌊 Max Historical Drawdown Peak-to-Trough Decline

The largest percentage decline experienced by the weighted portfolio from peak to subsequent trough over the lookback window. Represents historical stress-test tolerance.

Technical Momentum & Trend Indicators (Lay Guide)

Deterministic mathematical indicators calculated directly from 250 daily price bars in pure Python to ground analysis in price reality rather than LLM guesswork:

🎯 Relative Strength Index (RSI, 14-Day) Scale: 0 to 100

Measures price velocity and momentum. >70 (Overbought): The stock has rallied too fast; buyers are exhausted and the price is overdue for a rest or pullback. High risk for new buying. <30 (Oversold): Heavy panic selling; often marks potential bargain bottoming territory. 30–70: Healthy equilibrium.

🌊 MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence) 12, 26, 9 EMA

Compares short-term momentum (12-day EMA) to medium-term momentum (26-day EMA). Bullish Crossover: MACD line cuts up through the signal line—buyers taking aggressive control. Bearish Divergence: Stock makes a higher high, but MACD fails to confirm—early warning that upward momentum is exhausting.

⚡ Bollinger Bands & Volatility Squeeze 20 SMA ± 2 StdDev

Dynamic volatility envelopes. Volatility Squeeze: When the upper and lower bands pinch tightly together (bandwidth < 4.5%), volatility has collapsed to extreme lows—historically preceding an explosive breakout move. %B Indicator: 1.0 = at upper band (stretched), 0.0 = at lower band (discounted).

📏 50-Day & 200-Day Moving Averages Institutional Trendlines

The benchmarks used by institutional funds. 50 DMA: Intermediate trend health (price trading >10% above 50 DMA indicates short-term overextension). 200 DMA: Long-term dividing line between secular bull and bear markets.

🛡️ ATR-14 & Dynamic Trailing Stop Floor Average True Range

Measures normal daily dollar price fluctuation. Sentinel calculates a mathematically grounded trailing stop floor at Current Price - (2.0 × ATR) so that normal intraday volatility doesn't trigger false-alarm exits.

Retail Social Sentiment & Volume Velocity (Lay Guide)

Real-time intelligence from retail investor message streams (StockTwits & Reddit) combined with institutional trading volume to detect hype bubbles before mainstream financial media writes about them:

📱 StockTwits Retail Bullish % Direct Retail Stance

Directly calculates the ratio of retail posts tagged as Bullish vs. Bearish. >80% (Extreme Euphoria): Retail optimism is crowded. Often indicates a near-term top if unaccompanied by real institutional volume. <25% (Extreme Capitulation): Retail panic selling; often marks short-term counter-trend reversal opportunities.

🔥 Reddit Discussion Velocity r/wallstreetbets & r/stocks

Ingests recent discussion threads and velocity across Reddit investor communities. A sudden surge in thread mentions detects viral meme runs, gamma squeezes, or viral boycotts hours before they reach mainstream financial wires.

📊 Relative Volume (RVOL) Smart Money Confirmation

Compares live trading volume to the 20-day average volume (1.0x = normal). RVOL > 1.5x + High Bullish Sentiment: Confirms genuine institutional capital accumulation. RVOL < 0.7x + High Bullish Sentiment: Dangerous divergence—retail hype without institutional capital backing (bull trap warning).

⚖️ Dialectic Catalyst Examination Grounded Decision Logic

Sentinel forces every analysis to pit Fundamental Growth Drivers (Bull Case) against Technical Support Floors & Valuation Traps (Bear Case). Even if a business is fundamentally great, if RSI is overbought (>75) and retail hype is euphoric, the system warns you to Hold for a Pullback rather than chasing at resistance.
